Manifesto

The music data layer AI agents don't have yet.
And the search tool humans actually wanted.

There's no reliable music data layer for AI agents anywhere on the internet. Every AI asked about music guesses. Dig fixes that - fully open, no keys, built on the deepest music catalog that exists.

And because we're building on that same data, humans finally get the search tool Discogs should have built ten years ago. Deep search, proper mobile UX, crates from people with real taste, and every record connected to where you can stream it, buy it, or pay the artist direct.

Positioning
The music tool agents reach for. And the one humans actually wanted.
Problem

Music search is broken.
Everyone knows it. Nobody's fixed it.

  • 01 Discogs built the deepest music catalog on the internet. The search and mobile experience never kept up.
  • 02 Streaming is fast but shallow. You want the 1991 UK pressing and it gives you a 2019 remaster. No credits. No context. No way to tell the difference.
  • 03 Your curation lives in a notes app, a screenshot folder, a playlist called "check later" from 2022. None of it talks to anything else.
  • 04 AI tools make up catalog data. Ask about a specific pressing and they'll hallucinate a tracklist. Dig doesn't guess. Ever.
Platform

One record page. Everything connected.
The full catalog, the editorial, the API - all open.

Search lands you on a record. Credits, label, pressing info, linked artists and engineers. Click anything. Go deeper. Nothing is a dead end.

01

Search that goes deep

Artist, label, release, format, year, country, credit. Click an engineer, see everything they touched. Click a label, see their full catalog. Results tell you why they came up.

02

Crates

Record lists with intent. A DJ's selection. A label's back catalog. A radio show made permanent and linkable. Built by people who know, not an algorithm.

03

Stream it. Buy it.

Stream on Spotify or Apple. Buy on Discogs. Pay the artist on Bandcamp. We show confidence scores because the 1991 pressing and the 2019 remaster are not the same thing.

For agents

Fully open MCP. No keys, no signup. Point any agent at it and go. Dig is what the AI uses when it can't afford to guess.

Agent layer

There's no reliable music data layer for AI agents anywhere on the internet. Right now every AI asked about music guesses - confidently, fluently, wrongly. Dig fixes that. Fully open MCP, no keys, no signup. The first time any agent has somewhere real to go. ChatGPT, Claude, any LLM workflow - they call Dig, they get structured facts, provenance, the actual answer. That's not a feature. That's infrastructure that doesn't exist yet.
